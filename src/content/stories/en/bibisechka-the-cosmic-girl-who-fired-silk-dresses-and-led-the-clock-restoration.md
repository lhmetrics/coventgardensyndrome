## Bibisechka: The Cosmic Girl Who Fired Silk Dresses and Took Charge of the Grandfather Clock Restoration

### 🛸 A Far-Off Journey and the Fourth Girl

While every other tailed resident arrived at the Home spontaneously, driven by a sudden rush of emotion, Bibisechka's story was planned differently. Mum had been dreaming of a Don Sphynx for a long time — lightly, without pressure, filed under "someday, eventually." But then a photo of Angela (that's her official, passport name) appeared in the feed, and the dream demanded to be made real immediately.

Long negotiations with Dad followed. To make a considered decision, Dad took a diplomatic day to "think it over," secretly hoping Mum would change her mind. The strategy failed. Reasonable arguments didn't help, and the family simply had to make the long journey twice. That's how the fourth girl arrived at the Home, decisively and irreversibly tipping the balance of power toward matriarchy.

### 🧗 Lost in Translation, and Domestic Mountaineering

Bibisechka's intelligence didn't reveal itself right away. At first Mum even grew a little worried, suspecting that her alien daughter was growing up just a touch "slow." But her cosmic looks and absolute charm wrote off any mental slip-ups. Everything was forgiven her: a complete, categorical refusal of her passport name Angela (the sphynx deigned to answer only to the respectful "Bibisechka"), bold strolls across the dining table right between the vintage porcelain and crystal, swinging on the curtains, and testing the furniture's sturdiness with her claws.

Bibisechka's list of daily amusements came to include smuggling off with Mum's small necessary belongings to parts unknown, along with a rather peculiar taste in food. Unlike Bagheera, who sacredly guards her personal boundaries and thereby grants humans comfortable peace, Bibisechka operates as an absolute remora fish. She's obligated to appear in every single frame, monitor Mum's every movement, and literally merge with her into one being.

### 👗 Expelled from Couture Paradise, and the Clock Audit

Sphynxes are cosmic creatures, requiring care and tenderness like newborn infants. Caring for Bibisechka's comfort, Mum created an entire wardrobe for her: soft knit sweaters, premium silk dresses, and wonderful little outfits. But haute couture was rejected. Bibisechka firmly sabotaged the textiles and chose instead to parade around the Home completely naked, like Eve in the Garden of Eden.

She prefers to warm her bare, silky little rear exclusively against Mum's chest, and the accompanying inconvenience does nothing whatsoever to trouble her cosmic self. In her moments of rest, she can hilariously stretch out her back legs, looking like a ballerina warming up at the barre, and at night she always demands passage under the blanket.

Bibisechka holds a special passion for interior design and restoration work. Whenever restoration begins on a piece of antique furniture, or the old wooden cases of the grandfather clocks get taken apart, the sphynx immediately takes charge of the process. She personally conducts an audit of the empty dark depths, checks the quality of the UV lamp's work, and can sit for hours inside the disassembled clock mechanisms, supervising the craftsmen's hands with the air of time's chief architect.

### ⚔️ War of the Worlds

Bibisechka and Bagheera are absolute opposites, the Yin and Yang of the feline kingdom. The black panther can barely hold back her regal fury watching the hairless alien's eccentric antics. Bagheera genuinely considers the Home and Garden her own personal, inviolable residence, where she alone rules the world. Bibisechka simply exists outside these earthly conventions altogether. She is a cosmic girl, and neither humans nor panthers are granted the ability to guess what galactic signals are racing through her big, big-eared head at the exact moment she disrupts the Home's perfect geometry all over again.
