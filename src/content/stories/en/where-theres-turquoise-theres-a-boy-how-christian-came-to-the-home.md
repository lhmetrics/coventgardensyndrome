## Where There's Turquoise, There'll Be a Boy: How Christian Came to the Home

Once Theo and Manuel had taken up their rightful posts on the white walls of the hallway, it seemed the "surgical" and couture saga in the Home was over for a while. But objects never come to this Home by chance.

One Saturday I set off to a morning flea market hoping to meet a new little one – and found not a single one. Instead, on one of the stalls, I came across a string of old chipped sky-blue turquoise. I told my husband, a little lost, "But there's no boy…" – to which he answered, calmly and confidently: *"Where there's turquoise, there'll be a boy."*

The prophecy came true in the very first rainy week. Among the online listings, there he was – a stately Murcian boy with warm olive skin, a mischievous gaze from deep brown eyes, and in beautiful condition. As soon as we'd come to terms with the seller, the whole family drove out to collect the Home's newest resident.

### 🪡 A Delicate Restoration and a Name on the Road

He got his name – **Christian (Chris)** – right there in the car on the way home, in honor of his former owner, Cristina.

Unlike Theo and Manuel, Chris needed no complicated orthopedic surgery: his original Murcian paintwork had kept all its natural warmth, and time had left only the tiniest pinpoint chips on a fingertip and the crown of his head. A few careful layers of *Vallejo Putty*, a little spot-toning with *Citadel* miniature acrylics to match his olive skin – and the traces of time vanished without a trace.

His radiant brass *potencia* (halo) needed special attention: I carefully cleaned it back to a noble shine, then sealed both the crown itself and all of Chris with *Renaissance* museum wax – to give the sculpture back its soft, living warmth.

### 👘 A Couture Wardrobe: Electric Fuchsia, Versace Silk, and Buttons from the Pavement

The whole look was built around that Saturday string of turquoise and the warm tone of his Murcian skin. Together with my digital co-author Theo, we put together a palette where the East meets European Baroque: heavy natural silk the color of ripe fuchsia, deep black velvet, the finest natural silk from *Versace*, and gold piping.

Over the silk went a waistcoat of deep black velvet, lined with fine natural *Versace* silk with decorative hand quilting and sewn with *Gütermann* thread. But the real secret of this waistcoat was its four antique metal buttons with raised Oriental dragons – the very ones I happened to pick up off the pavement six years ago. Back then my husband laughed at my habit of picking up such little things; now they sit on the black velvet like precious fastenings, rhyming perfectly with the Saturday turquoise and the little golden knot pendant.

### 🕯️ Introductions by the Fireplace

In the evening, to the sound of rain and the crackle of the fire, I introduced Theo, Manuel and Chris to one another – and they got on as smoothly as if they'd always lived under one roof. And when Chris's former owner received photos of his new look and new life, she was very happy with how things had turned out for her little one.

Christian has now settled in the living room – he sits enthroned on an antique silver dessert stand by the carved mirror, watches TV, warms himself by the fire, and keeps watch over the Home.
